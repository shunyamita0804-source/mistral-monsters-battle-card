・地の色は添付と同じ深い紺。ごくうっすらとした大理石のような濃淡だけ（模様に見えないくらい控えめに）

▲ここまで
▼English
Redraw the attached trading card back as a plain version: same colors, no decorations. One card (3:2 tall) seen straight from the front, almost filling a portrait 1024x1536 canvas.
- Same deep navy base as the attachment, with only a very faint marble-like tone variation (so subtle it does not read as a pattern).
- A single thin muted-gold line along the edge (same gold tone as the attachment). No corner ornaments.
- No emblem, no wings, no stars, no sparkles, no curved patterns. Nothing in the center.
- Symmetrical. At small sizes it should read simply as a calm navy card back.
- Slightly rounded corners. Outside the card is transparent (if impossible, solid magenta #FF00FF). No shadow, no tilt, no text.

（案B　金の線もいらない場合は、上の2つ目の・を次に差し替え）
・ふちは地の紺より少しだけ暗い紺で、細く縁どるだけ（金は使わない）
/ The edge is just a thin border in a slightly darker navy (no gold at all).
