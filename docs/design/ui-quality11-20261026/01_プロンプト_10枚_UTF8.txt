▼English
Draw parts for the game's "Settings" screen, on a 1536x1024 canvas, no overlap. Interiors are plain (for text).
1 Section header bar: a long thin (8:1) bar with a small round icon base (muted-gold rim) at the left end and a thin gold line fading out to the right.
2 Section panel: a rounded panel (3:1), dark navy base, very thin muted-gold rim, small corner ornaments (volume and language items go inside).
3-6 Small icons (same size; they sit in the round base of 1): 3 = language (globe), 4 = sound (a small horn-shaped speaker), 5 = save backup (a scroll with a wax seal), 6 = how to play (an open book).
- Icons are muted-gold line art with navy shading, readable at about 24 px.

ゲームのタイトル画面に並べるボタンを描いてください。横長 1536×1024 に、上下に並べる（重ならないように）。中は文字を置くので無地。
・1 大きなボタン（続きから）：横:縦＝4.5:1 の、少し立派な札。くすんだ金の地に、紺の細い縁取り。左右の端に、添付のタイトルロゴと同じ形の小さなカード3枚の飾り。上の縁の真ん中に小さな星
・2 大きなボタン（最初から）：1 と同じ形と大きさで、地は深い紺、縁と飾りはくすんだ金

▼English
Draw the buttons for the game's title screen, stacked vertically on a 1536x1024 canvas, no overlap. Interiors are plain (for text).
1 Large button (Continue): a slightly grand plate (4.5:1), muted-gold base with a thin navy outline; at both ends, a small ornament of three cards shaped like the attached title logo; a small star at the top center of the rim.
2 Large button (New Game): same shape and size as 1, deep navy base with muted-gold rim and ornaments.
3 Small button (Settings): 3.5:1, smaller than 2 with simpler ornaments; deep navy base, muted-gold rim.
4 Pressed: same shape as 1, slightly darker and sunken overall.
- They sit on the attached title background (a bright daytime sky with floating island towns): don't let them stand out too much, and match the gold of the logo.
